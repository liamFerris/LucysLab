# Module 9: What are the bones of the maxillofacial skeleton?

*Revision of anatomy and radiographic anatomy*

## What you should be able to do

- Describe the maxilla in detail, including the main foramina and antra.
- Describe the mandible in detail, including its shape, internal structure and main foramina.
- Identify the main anatomical structures depicted radiographically.

## Studying this module

This subject is best understood by looking at the anatomical models and radiographs presented in the module. The printed revision handout contains no further notes for this module.
