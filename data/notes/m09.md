## Extra material

> **Note:** This section isn't in the printed handout. It was added to fill a gap in the notes, using standard dental radiography teaching.

### The maxilla

- Forms the upper jaw and carries the upper teeth.
- Contains the **maxillary antra (sinuses)**: air-filled spaces above the roots of the upper premolars and molars.
- On radiographs the antrum appears **radiolucent**, outlined by a thin radiopaque line.

### The mandible

- Forms the lower jaw and carries the lower teeth.
- **Internal structure:** a dense outer layer of **cortical bone** (radiopaque) surrounding inner **cancellous (trabecular) bone**, which has a finer, more radiolucent pattern.

### Main foramina and canals of the mandible

| Structure | Where it is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Mandibular foramen** | Inner (medial) surface of the ramus | Entrance to the inferior dental canal |
| **Inferior dental (mandibular) canal** | Runs through the body of the mandible | Carries the inferior alveolar nerve and vessels; appears as a radiolucent band outlined by thin radiopaque lines |
| **Mental foramen** | Outer surface of the mandible, near the apices of the lower premolars | Can look like a small radiolucency near a premolar apex on a periapical |
